PAYROLL CALCULATIONS
- Draft payroll reads the saved PIT bracket boundaries and rates, PIT sharing
  mode, employee-specific percentage when Custom is selected, SSO employee and
  company rates, SSO maximum contribution base, and employee enrollment flags.
- PIT sharing supports 50/50, employee 100%, company 100%, and Custom per employee.
  An invalid sharing setting or missing required custom percentage is blocked.
- The older startup migration no longer replaces saved PIT brackets merely
  because its formula94 marker is absent. New installations still receive the
  application's defaults. Review Settings before finalizing an actual payroll.
- Finalization retains the calculated results and settings snapshot. Later
  settings changes affect draft calculations, not finalized payroll amounts.
- The journal uses those payroll results and the payroll's recorded LAK exchange
  rates. Manually overridden journal amounts remain explicitly editable.

DEFAULT JOURNAL
Foreign-currency workers appear first (USD first), followed by LAK workers.
Foreign payroll:
  DR salary expense for gross salaries; CR each employee's personal clearing
  account for final payment, salary advance recovery and loan/MOU recovery.
  CR the USD Payroll Tax Bridge for the foreign employee PIT withholding total.
  DR LAK PIT Expense separately for company PIT and employee PIT equivalents.
  CR LAK PIT Payable for the combined foreign PIT amount.
Local payroll:
  DR salary expense for gross salaries; CR each employee's personal clearing
  account for final payment and applicable recoveries.
  CR PIT Payable for employee withholding.
  DR PIT Expense for the company share; CR PIT Payable for that company share.
  Local PIT Expense is not doubled: the employee withholding already credits
  PIT Payable. Both credits together equal the total PIT.
SSO uses the saved rates and ceiling when the employee is enrolled:
  Employee withholding credits SSO Payable; company SSO debits SSO Expense and
  credits SSO Payable. Zero-value lines are omitted.

REVIEW AND POST
Open a payroll and choose Payroll template / Journal.
- The default resolves accounts against your actual chart and configured PIT /
  SSO payable accounts. Existing custom templates remain intact. Use default
  template replaces those rules only after confirmation.
- Review each employee's personal clearing account in their salary currency.
- Change the posting account, DR/CR, amount source, description, employee filter
  and grouping. Add, remove or reorder lines. Blank override fields calculate
  automatically; a manual amount is in the selected account's currency.
- Review the live journal preview and separate currency balances. Save template
  retains the rules and mappings with that payroll.
- Finalize payroll before Prepare journal. Preparation opens an editable Double
  Entry draft in Transactions. Review it, then choose Post Payroll Journal.
- Payroll and journal retain separate linked IDs. Salary History includes the
  payroll only after its journal posting is confirmed.
- A pending/interrupted posting is protected from blind retry. Use Refresh
  posting status and review Journal History to confirm its outcome.

ACCOUNT MAPPINGS THAT NEED YOUR REVIEW
Your supplied chart contains a LAK Rounding Difference account but no USD
Rounding Difference account. If conversion creates a small USD difference,
select an appropriate existing USD rounding account in the rounding row.
The app does not use a LAK account for a USD difference or invent an account.
Foreign SSO bridge accounts and reimbursement expense accounts require explicit
selection when those amounts apply. Missing accounts and currency mismatches
are shown in the preview and block journal preparation.
