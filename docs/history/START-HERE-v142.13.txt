OON JAI ACCOUNTING v142.13 — COMPLETE APPLICATION
Includes all v142.12 changes and the bundled Restaurant workspace.

INSTALL
Extract the whole ZIP and keep all folders together. Open the root index.html.
For a hosted installation, upload the complete extracted structure and reload.
No new SQL is required for this update. Earlier login and Restaurant setup
requirements remain as described in START-HERE-v142.12.txt. Do not reset data.

SUB-ACCOUNTS
The table now uses the main Chart of Accounts fitted layout and existing table
styling. Parent accounts, codes, names and descriptions wrap inside the page;
rows grow to show complete text. The existing row action menu is retained.

PAYROLL VOUCHERS ONLY
The DRAFT — NOT FINALIZED label is removed from newly generated vouchers.
The payroll run's status and finalization workflow are unchanged.
The SAMPLE — NOT FOR PAYMENT label remains on sample vouchers.

Choose two or three distinct signatories before opening vouchers in Documents.
The employee is always Received by, at the end of the signature row.
For your own voucher, the first role defaults to Verified by and the other
signer's name is blank for you to select. For another employee, Prepared by
initially uses your name. The first role can be Prepared by or Verified by;
a two-person voucher can also use Approved by. Three-person vouchers add a
separate Approved by signer. No receiver may also be another signer, and the
first signer and approver must differ.

Identity checks use linked user IDs, email addresses and normalized full names,
including middle initials. Keep employee and user identities consistent; the
system cannot reliably match unrelated aliases without a shared identifier.
Choose the actual people who will sign. Names label unsigned signature lines
and do not record an approval. Printing lets you review or change these choices.
Other reports retain their existing signatory flow.
