SINGLE ENTRY
Money Out is the default direction for a new row:
  DR affected/category account; CR source/payment account.
Money In reverses those sides:
  DR source/payment account; CR affected/category account.
Choose both accounts, enter the amount and the general memo, then Post Entry.
The existing posting workflow writes the matching journal lines. Pending saves
are not included in ledger totals before the server confirms them.

Add creates another pair. Multiple enables an individual date per simple row.
Both accounts in a simple row must have the same currency and be different.
Use Double Entry for currency bridging or complex split transactions. Switching
preserves draft lines, including unfinished values; an unchanged simple draft
also retains its chosen Money In / Money Out direction when switched back.
Existing edits, prepared payroll and approval drafts stay in Double Entry.

An ordinary balanced pair can be displayed as Money Out with its credited account
as source; review the selected accounts when converting a manually entered pair.
Multiple-date posting uses the existing sequential posting workflow. If a later
transaction fails, check Active Journal for earlier successful dates before retrying.
