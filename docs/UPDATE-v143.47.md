# v143.47 — Returned submissions and repeat submissions

Returned reports open directly in the editable Active Journal and appear before ordinary entries. Review badges identify submitted and returned reports. Single-entry snapshots no longer crash journal rendering. Each sub-user retains separate assigned funds; labels no longer describe team records or team funds.

Sub-users can submit several reports within one month. A new draft is independent of submitted and returned reports. Explicit correction saves add new lines only to the chosen returned report, and each submission confirms reconciliation again. Main-book month-end restrictions remain in place.

Reopen is disabled for already-open periods. Progress appears at the top center. Desktop workspace actions move to the right of the Sub-users header; recent amounts stay on one line. Settings and sub-user strips cannot appear in Finalization.

Database installer: setup/INSTALL-RETURNED-SUBMISSIONS-v143.47.sql. Existing transactions and submissions are preserved. Validation includes a rolled-back database round trip and desktop/tablet/phone browser checks.
