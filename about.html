Oon Jai Accounting

Oon Jai Marketplace staff use this app to record transactions, reconcile accounts and keep supporting documents with their journal entries.

Receipt scanning

Staff can photograph receipts on a phone, review the pages and save a PDF. A company administrator connects Google Drive and selects the receipt folder. Staff with scanner access can then file documents using the company connection.

Google Sheets and Drive

The app checks the connected Google email address so the administrator can confirm the account. Drive access allows the app to work with existing folders and save and retrieve documents. When Sheets is connected, Accounting can read or update the spreadsheets configured for accounting records and archives.
