Oon Jai Accounting privacy policy

This policy describes how Oon Jai Marketplace’s accounting app handles data when staff use the receipt scanner or connect a Google account for company records.

Data the app accesses

When an administrator connects Google, the app checks the account’s verified email address to identify the account. The Drive connection requests access to existing files and folders so the app can locate the selected destination, save and open PDFs, and manage folders and accounting archives. It uses file and folder identifiers, names, links, versions and checksums to place and verify files.

When the administrator connects Google Sheets, the app can read and write configured spreadsheets for accounting functions such as journal imports, exports and archives. Those sheets can contain transaction descriptions, dates, amounts and other records selected for the integration.

Where data goes and who can see it

Google hosts the files and spreadsheets saved to the company account. Google Drive’s sharing settings govern access to the originals. The app uses Supabase for its accounting records, document links, private previews and server-side connection credentials. Accounting permissions govern which signed-in staff can open linked previews. Google access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted so the app can renew the connection.

The scanner keeps encrypted drafts on the device. Saving a PDF locally creates a separate file. Saving or linking a document online sends the document or its previews and reference information to the selected storage service and the accounting workspace as needed for that action.

Managing access and stored copies

The connected account holder can remove the app’s access from Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future use of that authorization. Existing PDFs, spreadsheet records, linked previews and device drafts have separate storage locations. Staff can discard drafts in the scanner and manage exported files in their storage location.

For questions or requests to access, correct or delete information held in the accounting workspace, email oonjaimarketplace@gmail.com. Identify the relevant record by its reference or date.
